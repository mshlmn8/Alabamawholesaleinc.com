What those files show (each file looked at). #205, #248 and #270 were the wrong product and were replaced on 2026-11-07 with licensed photos of the product the row sells. The brand sites blocked a direct download, so these three come from Wikimedia Commons rather than a retailer CDN. #249 and #88 are still the packs described here:
- #205 Pepsi, `p205-pepsi.jpg`: a 355 mL Pepsi can, the classic blue can with the red, white and blue globe. Public-domain photo by Chris Woodrich. The earlier file was an Aquafina 32-pack of water.
- #270 Monster Energy, `p270-monster-energy.jpg`: the original green Monster Energy can (green claw, MONSTER ENERGY). CC0 photo by TurnOnTheNight. The earlier file was two Softsoap Aquarium hand-soap bottles. This can is the China-market printing, so the net-content line is in Chinese.
- #248 Coca-Cola, `p248-coke.jpg`: a regular red Coca-Cola can, 50 cl, Italy, held in a hand. Not Diet Coke. CC BY-SA 3.0 photo by M0tty; the product page credits it (`src/data/photoCredits.js`). The earlier file was a Diet Coke 12-pack. The re-encoded file stays under CC BY-SA 3.0.
- #249 Sprite, `p249-sprite.jpg`: a Sprite lemon-lime 12-pack carton and one limited-edition can printed "Insane in the Membrane", with the retailer's "12 fl oz 12pk" banner. It is Sprite, not a single standard can. Still the 2026-09-27 retailer file.
- #88 Tropical Fantasy, `p88-tropical-fantasy.jpg`: one bottle of Tropical Fantasy Apple premium juice cocktail, with a $1.00 price mark on the label. Not Mean Green Lemonade, and Apple is not one of the row's eight flavors (AW-142). Still the 2026-09-27 retailer file.
